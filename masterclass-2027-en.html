THE MASTERCLASS

From piano sessions to the orchestra

Piano sessions on 6–7 July are followed by orchestral sessions on 8–9 July, then the final rehearsal and concert on 10 July. Each Active participant is guaranteed 120 minutes of podium time. Final-concert rehearsal and performance time are additional and do not count toward these 120 minutes.

PROFESSOR

Yunsung Chang

Professor of Conducting, Seoul National University

Yunsung Chang studied composition at Seoul Arts High School and Seoul National University and completed conducting coursework at its graduate school. While studying at the Vienna music university, he won second prize at the first Prokofiev International Conducting Competition in St Petersburg in 1993, followed by second prize, with no first prize awarded, at the Tokyo International Conducting Competition in 1997. He has held conducting posts with orchestras in Korea and Japan and is Professor of Conducting at Seoul National University.

Yunsung Chang studied composition at Seoul Arts High School and Seoul National University and completed coursework in conducting at the university’s graduate school. While studying at the University of Music and Performing Arts Vienna, he won second prize at the inaugural Prokofiev International Conducting Competition in St Petersburg in 1993 and second prize, with no first prize awarded, at the Tokyo International Conducting Competition in 1997.

He has conducted the St Petersburg Philharmonic, St Petersburg Symphony and Camerata St Petersburg in Russia; Tokyo Symphony, Osaka Philharmonic and Sendai Philharmonic in Japan; Wiener Concert-Verein and Klagenfurt Symphony in Austria; Robert Schumann Philharmonic Chemnitz and Nuremberg Symphony in Germany; Rome Symphony in Italy; Orquesta Clásica Santa Cecilia in Madrid, Spain; MÁV Symphony Orchestra in Hungary; Prague Radio Symphony, Prague Symphony, Brno Symphony and Janáček Philharmonic in the Czech Republic; Kraków Radio Symphony in Poland; Kharkiv Symphony in Ukraine; and Belgrade Symphony in Serbia.

His touring engagements include four German cities with the Nuremberg Symphony, Japan with the KBS Symphony Orchestra, Korea with the Mariinsky Opera Orchestra and Camerata St Petersburg, and Klagenfurt and St Pölten in Austria with the Brno Philharmonic and Janáček Philharmonic. He has conducted ballet performances with Universal Ballet at Sadler’s Wells in London, Lyric Opera of Chicago and Vancouver’s Queen Elizabeth Theatre, as well as Seoul Metropolitan Opera tours to Tokyo and Osaka. His touring repertoire spans symphonic music, ballet and opera.

In 1995, he conducted the European premiere of Penderecki’s Symphony No. 5, “KOREA,” in Kraków. His Korean premieres include Schoenberg’s Gurre-Lieder, Casella’s Symphony No. 2 and Bloch’s Symphony in C-sharp minor. He has also premiered numerous works by Korean composers, maintaining a broad repertoire from the Classical period to modern and contemporary music.

Beginning with Mozart’s L’oca del Cairo at Austria’s Neustudio Bühne, his opera repertoire includes Mozart’s Le nozze di Figaro, Don Giovanni, Così fan tutte and Die Zauberflöte; Rossini’s La cambiale di matrimonio and Il barbiere di Siviglia; Humperdinck’s Hänsel und Gretel; Donizetti’s L’elisir d’amore; Verdi’s Rigoletto, La traviata, La forza del destino and Nabucco; Puccini’s Il trittico, Madama Butterfly, La bohème and Tosca; Bizet’s Carmen; Britten’s A Midsummer Night’s Dream; and Honegger’s Jeanne d’Arc au bûcher.

He has served as Principal Conductor of the Ulsan, Changwon and Daejeon symphony orchestras and the Bucheon Philharmonic Orchestra; Resident Conductor of Japan’s Osaka College Opera House; Principal Guest Conductor of the Seoul Philharmonic Orchestra; and Resident Conductor of the Gunpo Prime Philharmonic Orchestra. He has released recordings on Japan’s Camerata label. He is currently Professor of Conducting in the Department of Composition at Seoul National University’s College of Music.

ACTIVE · 10

Active · Conducting participants

  • 10 places. All nationalities; born on or before 31 December 2008.
  • Open to conducting majors and musicians interested in conducting; non-majors may also apply.
  • Choir, church choir, school orchestra and community orchestra conductors may apply.
  • Applicants preparing for or applying to Korean university entrance examinations are not eligible.
  • Direct coaching by Professor Yunsung Chang with piano and orchestra
  • 120 guaranteed podium minutes: 60 with piano + 60 with orchestra
  • Every Active participant has an opportunity to conduct in the final concert; certificate provided

Application, selection & registration

  1. Submit the online form and a conducting-video link
  2. Application and video review → selection results and payment instructions for accepted applicants by email within two weeks of receipt
  3. Pay within five days of the acceptance notification
  4. Final registration is confirmed in order of payment receipt

Acceptance does not constitute final registration. Enrollment closes once 10 paid registrations are confirmed, even if other payment deadlines have not passed. Unpaid accepted applicants will be notified and no further payments accepted. Eligibility to register expires if payment is not made by the deadline.

Audition video

  • Orchestra or piano conducting footage filmed within the last three years
  • The conductor’s gestures must be clearly visible; a frontal or near-frontal view is recommended.
  • Submit only a public or unlisted YouTube link. Private videos and direct file submissions are not accepted.
  • You may submit up to two audition videos. The minimum video duration is five minutes. Edited videos are accepted.
